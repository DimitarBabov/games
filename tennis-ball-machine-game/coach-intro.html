Ball Machine Quest

We turned George's Proton ball machine practice into a game: 100 levels in 10 worlds, 10 balls per level, stars for balls that land in a target. We'd love your opinion.

How it's built

  • Worlds follow junior prioritiesConsistency → depth → movement → direction → spin, then return of serve, net, passing and match patterns. That covers all five game situations.
  • Same ball → pattern → random, inside every worldLevels 1–3 repeat one ball, 4–6 are patterns, 7–9 are random, 10 is a 20-ball boss. Studies suggest 10-year-olds start gaining from random practice (Farrow & Maschette).
  • Hard but doableAbout 7 of 10 in the target to pass. ★★★ on the first try skips a level; three misses drops him back one.
  • The machine movesCorners for real crosscourt rallies, service line for fast volleys, net as the opponent for passing shots, and the Apex stand to serve.
