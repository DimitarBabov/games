Ball Machine Quest

We turned George's Proton ball machine practice into a game: 100 levels in 10 worlds, 10 balls per level, stars for balls that land in a target. We'd love your opinion.

How the levels are built

  • One change at a timeSpeed (60 → 90 km/h), spin, ball placement, order, or target size.
  • Same ball → pattern → randomRepetition first, then footwork patterns, then random order for match transfer.
  • Pass at about 7 out of 10Kids learn best at 60–80% success. Boss level every 10th level.
  • The target shrinksWhole court → past the service line → one half → 3 × 3 m corner boxes.

Example: level 3

Machine alternates forehand and backhand, 60 km/h, flat, every 6 seconds. George must land 7 of 10 anywhere in the court for one star.
